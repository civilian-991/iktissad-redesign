# Iktissad — Design Context

This file is the canonical brief for any design work on Iktissad. Load it before invoking design skills (`/polish`, `/critique`, `/typeset`, etc.). Do not infer brand context from the codebase — read this.

## Design Context

### Users

Gulf and Levant business decision-makers (30–55): analysts, executives, policy advisors, finance professionals, journalists, academics. Bilingual but Arabic-primary; English is for cross-referencing and international reach.

**Reading context:**
- Desktop during the workday → scanning analysis between meetings, opening 2–4 articles in tabs.
- Phone/tablet in the evening → long-form reading, magazine spreads, opinion columns.
- Subscribers expect editorial weight in exchange for the paywall.

### Job-to-be-done

Authoritative analysis and considered commentary first; market context second. Iktissad answers *why*, not *what just happened*. The reader is not here to scan tickers — they are here to understand the shape of a story.

### Brand Personality

Three words: **Considered. Principled. Warm.**

- **Considered** — slow takes, edited prose, no hot-take energy. The pace of motion, the spacing, the type sizing should all read as unhurried.
- **Principled** — editorial spine, named bylines, opinions earn their place. Authority comes from restraint, not volume.
- **Warm** — cream paper and amber gold, not cold cyan and chrome. The brand has the temperature of newsprint and brass.

Emotional target: the reader should feel *respected* (their time, their intelligence, their attention) and *grounded* (this is reliable, this is settled).

### Aesthetic Direction

**Reference points:**
- Spatial restraint of **ft.com** (whitespace, masthead authority, asymmetric grids).
- Editorial conviction of **The Economist** (named-author voice, pull-quotes that matter, no clutter).
- Executed in **Arabic script with confidence** — Tajawal sized up, generous leading, real hierarchy. Arabic is the primary citizen, not a translation layer.

**Anti-references (explicit):**
- **NOT RT Arabic** — the red/black aggression, oversized photo crops, political-tabloid energy.
- **NOT Al Jazeera / Al Arabiya** — dense breaking-news clutter, every section competing for attention, too many CTAs.
- **NOT Bloomberg terminal** — data-density-as-aesthetic, monospace cosplay, decorative sparklines.
- **NOT a stock CMS theme** — generic card grids, identical featured-image-plus-headline-plus-excerpt blocks repeating forever.

**Theme:** **Light-only.** Cream/paper base. No dark mode. Optional evening *reading mode* may warm the cream slightly, but no near-black backgrounds with glowing gold — that breaks the brand's temperature.

### Committed Design Tokens (already in `src/app/globals.css`)

These are not suggestions — they are the system. Use them.

- **Palette:** deep teal-navy `#183B4E` (brand) + warm amber gold `#DDA853` (accent) + warm cream/paper neutrals. Pure black and pure white are forbidden — always tint warm.
- **Typography:** Tajawal (Arabic display + body) + Playfair Display (English accent / pull-quotes / masthead). Body 18px, line-height 1.85 in Arabic; 1rem / 1.6 in English.
- **Radii:** sharp — 2–6px max. No rounded-rectangle softness; this is print-inflected, not app-inflected.
- **Shadows:** warm, never blue-tinted. `rgba(24, 59, 78, 0.05–0.14)`.
- **Motion:** slow and deliberate. `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)` (editorial), 0.4–0.7s durations for content reveals. No bounce, no elastic.
- **Spacing:** generous. `--spacing-section: 6rem`. Vary spacing for hierarchy — do not apply the same padding everywhere.

### Design Principles

1. **Restraint over decoration.** When in doubt, remove. The cream paper itself is the design; ornament earns its place.
2. **Hierarchy through space and weight, not color.** Color is rare and meaningful — the amber gold is an accent, not a background.
3. **Arabic-first typography.** Sizing, leading, and rhythm are tuned for Tajawal first. English is the visitor.
4. **The magazine moment.** The magazine-issue lockup, the article masthead, and the pull-quote are the three trust moments. Spend disproportionate care there.
5. **No AI tells.** No gradient text, no colored side-stripes on cards, no glassmorphism, no cyan-on-dark, no sparkline decoration, no card-in-a-card. See `<absolute_bans>` in the impeccable skill.

### Accessibility

- WCAG AA contrast minimum on all body text.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — reveal/stagger animations must collapse to instant.
- Logical CSS properties everywhere (`start-*` / `end-*` / `ms-*` / `me-*`) — already enforced in Phase 8.1, do not regress.
- Bidi isolation (`<bdi>`) on embedded English/numbers inside Arabic prose — already implemented in `src/lib/i18n/format.ts`.
